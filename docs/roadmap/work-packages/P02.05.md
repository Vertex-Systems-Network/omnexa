# P02.05 — RBAC Foundation

- Phase: `P02`
- State: `active`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.authorization`
- Depends on: `P02.04`
- Blocks: `P02.06`–`P02.10`
- Change class: `B`

> After this governed closure merges, `kernel_code_authorized=true` only for P02.05. `business_feature_code_authorized=false` remains locked.

## Objective

Create deny-by-default role/permission composition on trusted identity, tenant and organization context without role-name shortcuts or relationship-policy behavior reserved for P02.06.

## In scope

- stable permission identifiers and governed capability-oriented permission semantics;
- Role as a permission composition, not a bypass identity;
- tenant/organization scoped role definitions/assignments where authorized;
- assignment/revocation lifecycle and deterministic evaluation of direct RBAC grants;
- privileged role/permission mutation checks and classification-safe audit hooks;
- allow, deny and privilege-escalation negative tests.

## Out of scope

- object/relationship/context policy (`P02.06`);
- MFA/passkey requirements (`P02.07`);
- service accounts/API credential scope (`P02.08`);
- P03 module permission registration;
- business-module permissions or hidden super-admin logic.

## Security and architecture invariants

- `admin`, `owner`, `superuser` or any role name never means platform bypass;
- protected mutations authorize server-side;
- role assignment itself is privileged and auditable;
- permission scope cannot cross tenant/org boundaries by identifier substitution;
- owner is `kernel.authorization`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. roles compose permissions deterministically and deny by default.
2. allowed role behavior passes and unassigned/unauthorized behavior fails closed.
3. role/permission assignment cannot escalate authority without required capability.
4. tenant/org scope substitution cannot widen role authority.
5. privileged changes produce safe audit metadata through the existing audit boundary where applicable.
6. no relationship-policy, module-runtime or business permission scope is pulled forward.
7. migrations, Go quality and prior regressions pass as applicable.
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.

## Completion evidence

Record exact implementation/CI and authorization-negative evidence only after observed execution. Activation is not completion evidence.

## State transition

`planned -> active -> verification -> done`; current state is `active`. Separate closure only may activate P02.06.
