# P02.01 — Principal & User Identity Foundation

- Phase: `P02`
- State: `active`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.identity`
- Depends on: `P01 done`, `P02 entry gate satisfied`
- Blocks: `P02.02`–`P02.10`

> `kernel_code_authorized=true` only for P02.01 after this governed activation merges. `business_feature_code_authorized=false` remains locked.

## Objective

Establish the canonical human identity/principal model and lifecycle required by later tenancy, authentication and authorization packages without creating a business Person model or pulling authentication/session behavior forward.

## In scope

- stable UUIDv7 identity/principal identifiers;
- human `User` identity lifecycle and explicit lifecycle states;
- principal-type vocabulary/extensibility required by the Security Standard;
- classification-safe identity attributes needed before tenancy/authentication;
- repository/persistence boundary owned only by `kernel.identity` where implementation requires persistence;
- deterministic constructors/state transitions and disclosure-safe errors;
- positive/negative tests and applicable fresh/upgrade migration evidence.

## Out of scope

- business `Person` / Party records;
- tenant lifecycle or membership authority (`P02.02`);
- organization hierarchy (`P02.03`);
- authentication credentials/sessions (`P02.04`);
- roles/policies (`P02.05-P02.06`);
- MFA/passkeys (`P02.07`);
- service-account/API credential lifecycle (`P02.08`);
- business modules, P03 module runtime, deployment or AI/model/agent behavior.

## Security and architecture invariants

- User is authentication identity, never a substitute for business Person.
- Non-human identities are not fake human users.
- identity attributes never imply authorization by themselves.
- secrets/authentication material are not introduced by this package.
- owner is `kernel.identity`; no cross-domain private-table writes.
- tenant claims are not yet authority.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Canonical User/principal identity and lifecycle semantics are deterministic and owner-bounded.
2. UUIDv7 and canonical error/time conventions are reused rather than duplicated.
3. invalid lifecycle transitions fail closed.
4. User/Person separation is explicitly tested/documented.
5. no authentication/session/tenant/RBAC/service-account authority is introduced early.
6. schema changes, if any, pass fresh and supported-upgrade migration evidence.
7. repository Go quality and all P01 regressions remain green.
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
