# P02.03 — Organization Hierarchy & Scoped Memberships

- Phase: `P02`
- State: `active`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.organization`
- Depends on: `P02.02`
- Blocks: `P02.04`–`P02.10`

> After this governed closure merges, `kernel_code_authorized=true` only for P02.03. `business_feature_code_authorized=false` remains locked.

## Objective

Establish tenant-contained organization structure and scoped membership relationships without merging access hierarchy with business Party records.

## In scope

- Organization, Legal Entity, Business Unit, Branch, Team and Location hierarchy semantics required by frozen architecture;
- tenant-bound parent/child validation and scoped membership relationships;
- deterministic hierarchy traversal/validation with cycle and cross-tenant rejection;
- organization/sub-scope context primitives for later policy evaluation;
- classification-safe persistence and fresh/upgrade migration evidence where needed.

## Out of scope

- business Party/Person/Customer/Supplier models;
- authentication/session implementation (`P02.04`);
- role/policy enforcement beyond relationship primitives (`P02.05-P02.06`);
- MFA/passkeys (`P02.07`);
- service-account/API credential lifecycle (`P02.08`);
- tenant-scoped settings (`P02.09`);
- P02 phase-exit audit product behavior (`P02.10`);
- HR employment, warehouse/store business behavior, CRM or other modules;
- P03+, module runtime, business UI/features, deployment/Kubernetes authority or AI/model/agent runtime.

## Security and architecture invariants

- Tenant membership alone does not authorize every organization/sub-scope.
- Organization is not Party Organization.
- hierarchy relationships never cross tenant boundaries.
- trusted tenant context from P02.02 is the enclosing isolation boundary and cannot be bypassed by organization input.
- no ambiguous `company`/`workspace` synonym replaces canonical Tenant/Organization semantics.
- owner is `kernel.organization`; no cross-domain private-table writes are permitted.

## Acceptance criteria

1. hierarchy types and ownership follow glossary/constitution.
2. valid same-tenant hierarchy/membership works; cross-tenant parent/membership fails closed.
3. cycles and invalid scope transitions are rejected deterministically.
4. organization scope is available for later authorization without granting authority itself.
5. no business Person/Party/HR behavior is introduced.
6. applicable fresh/upgrade migrations, Go quality and prior regressions pass.
7. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.

## Completion evidence

Exact implementation/head/run/job and hierarchy-isolation/migration/security evidence is recorded only after observed execution. Activation is not completion evidence.

## State transition

`planned -> active -> verification -> done`; current state is `active`. Only a separate closure after observed completion evidence may activate P02.04.
