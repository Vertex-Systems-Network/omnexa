## Objective

Provide the protected transport/envelope boundary for future security and business audit records so privileged actions can emit tamper-evident, classification-aware audit facts without conflating them with ordinary logs or prematurely defining domain audit events.

## In scope

- stable audit record envelope aligned with P00 security and data-classification standards;
- actor/action/target/scope/outcome/correlation/reason/approval metadata slots without implementing P02 identities;
- append-oriented sink interface and explicit required-audit failure semantics;
- classification/redaction enforcement boundary;
- immutable UUIDv7/timestamp conventions;
- explicit impersonation/privileged-action metadata representation;
- deterministic local/test sink;
- bounded transport-health observability without logging protected payloads;
- P01.01-P01.10 completed-package regression preservation;
- repository-wide Go quality verification;
- GitHub-hosted G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence.

## Out of scope

- P02 user/tenant/role audit catalog or identity implementation;
- business-domain audit event definitions;
- compliance export/reporting UI;
- long-term legal retention/hold implementation;
- replacing domain events with audit records;
- P01.12 developer CLI;
- durable messaging/event fabric or outbox/inbox pull-forward;
- later business modules or AI/model/agent behavior.

## Security & reliability invariants

Protected audit is separate from ordinary logs. Audit failure cannot silently claim success for operations whose policy requires durable audit; callers receive explicit failure/degradation semantics. Records must not contain secrets/auth equivalents by default. Audit write access does not imply audit read/export authority. Actor/scope identifiers are descriptive metadata during P01.11 and do not grant authority or establish P02 tenancy/identity.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Envelope requires stable actor/action/target/scope/outcome/correlation fields appropriate to the operation.
2. Audit records are append-oriented and immutable through the public interface.
3. Negative tests reject secret/prohibited payload fields and unsafe classification behavior.
4. Required-audit sink failure propagates explicit failure rather than silently dropping the record.
5. Impersonation/reason/approval metadata can be represented without P02 identity implementation.
6. Audit and application logging remain separate contracts; health telemetry exposes no protected payload.
7. Completed P01.01-P01.10 regression verifiers remain PASS.
8. Repository-wide Go quality remains PASS.
9. Applicable G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence is PASS on GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.

## Completion evidence

Record envelope/schema and deterministic-sink tests, classification/redaction and required-sink-failure negatives, exact source SHA, workflow run/job, executing runner, P01.01-P01.10 regressions and repository Go-quality evidence.

Required `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT RUN` or `N/A` evidence cannot be relabeled PASS.
