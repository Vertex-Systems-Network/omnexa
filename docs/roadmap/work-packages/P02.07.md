## Objective

Add strong-authentication factor/challenge/recovery architecture on the established identity/session/policy foundations, with passkey-ready semantics and RESTRICTED secret handling.

## In scope

- MFA factor enrollment/verification/removal lifecycle;
- passkey/WebAuthn-ready credential/challenge contract consistent with platform security requirements;
- approved additional factor semantics where implemented;
- recovery-code lifecycle with one-way/secure handling;
- step-up/strong-auth policy hooks for privileged operations;
- replay/expiry/binding validation for authentication challenges;
- synthetic fixtures and no-secret telemetry/audit behavior.

## Out of scope

- enterprise SSO/SAML/SCIM (`P24`);
- service accounts/API credentials (`P02.08`);
- business portal UI;
- custom cryptographic algorithms or private-key management outside approved platform primitives.

## Security and architecture invariants

- factor secrets, recovery codes and authentication-equivalent material are `RESTRICTED`;
- no secret values in logs/traces/errors/audit payloads;
- challenge expiry/replay and user/session binding fail closed;
- factor removal/security-policy change may trigger session invalidation according to policy;
- strong authentication never replaces authorization.

## Acceptance criteria

1. enrollment/verification/removal lifecycle is deterministic and policy-bound.
2. expired/replayed/wrong-principal challenges fail closed.
3. recovery material is not reversibly exposed after issuance where policy forbids it.
4. privileged step-up hooks can require stronger authentication without bypassing authorization.
5. secret-scanning/redaction negative tests prove no factor material leaks.
6. no SSO/service-account/business scope is pulled forward.
7. migrations, Go quality and prior regressions pass as applicable.
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
