## Objective

Implement authentication and interactive session lifecycle on the canonical identity/tenant/organization foundations without treating authentication as authorization.

## In scope

- authentication mechanism boundary and approved adaptive password hashing where passwords are supported;
- session/access/refresh credential model with explicit expiry, rotation and revocation;
- short-lived access credentials relative to refresh/session credentials;
- device/session inventory semantics where supported;
- invalidation after material account/security changes according to policy;
- tenant/organization context re-authorization rather than trusting stale client context;
- disclosure-safe authentication failures and synthetic security fixtures;
- audit hooks for security-significant lifecycle changes without secret payloads.

## Out of scope

- RBAC/policy decisions (`P02.05-P02.06`);
- MFA/passkey factors (`P02.07`);
- service accounts/API credentials (`P02.08`);
- SAML/SCIM/enterprise SSO (`P24`);
- business login portals/UI or business features.

## Security and architecture invariants

- authentication proves identity; it does not grant business authority;
- passwords are never plaintext or reversibly stored;
- refresh/session secrets are `RESTRICTED` and never logged;
- bearer possession does not bypass current authorization/policy state;
- reset/recovery flows are authentication flows, not ordinary profile updates.

## Acceptance criteria

1. valid authentication/session issuance follows explicit lifecycle/expiry rules.
2. invalid credentials do not disclose unnecessary account existence/details.
3. rotation/revocation and required session invalidation are deterministic and tested.
4. tokens/secrets are absent from logs, traces, errors and audit payloads.
5. stale tenant/org context cannot become authorization authority.
6. no RBAC/MFA/service-account/business scope is pulled forward.
7. applicable fresh/upgrade migrations, Go quality and prior regressions pass.
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
