# P02.06 — Relationship / Context-Aware Authorization

- Phase: `P02`
- State: `active`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.authorization`
- Depends on: `P02.05`
- Blocks: `P02.07`–`P02.10`
- Change class: `B`

> `kernel_code_authorized=true` only for P02.06 after this closure merges and protected-main state is verified. `business_feature_code_authorized=false` remains locked.

## Objective

Combine RBAC with trusted tenant/organization relationships, object scope and contextual policy checks at governed capability boundaries.

## In scope

- relationship/context policy evaluation layered on P02.05 RBAC;
- tenant, organization and object-scope relationships;
- capability-bound authorization decisions with deny-by-default semantics;
- contextual conditions that cannot grant authority outside the principal's valid relationships;
- field/export distinction hooks where broader read authority must not imply sensitive-field/export authority;
- disclosure-safe deny behavior and material authorization audit hooks;
- same-scope allow plus wrong-tenant/wrong-org/wrong-object negative tests.

## Out of scope

- P03 module permission registration/capability registry;
- business-domain object policies not yet owned by an active domain;
- support impersonation product surface;
- AI/tool permission execution;
- alternative authentication stacks.

## Security and architecture invariants

- authorization = RBAC + relationships + context/policy + capability boundaries;
- client IDs/tenant IDs/object IDs are references, never authority;
- tenant membership alone is insufficient for all child scopes;
- field/export authorization may be stricter than ordinary resource read;
- background/internal callers do not bypass policy by being inside the process.

## Acceptance criteria

1. a valid relationship/context plus required permission allows the governed action.
2. wrong tenant, wrong organization, wrong object relationship and missing permission deny deterministically.
3. role names and internal-call origin cannot bypass policy.
4. contextual rules never widen beyond trusted principal/scope relationships.
5. material denials/privileged decisions are auditable without leaking sensitive data where applicable.
6. no P03/business/AI authorization surface is introduced.
7. Go quality, migrations where affected and all prior regressions pass.
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
