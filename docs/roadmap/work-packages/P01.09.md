## Objective

Provide minimal background job and scheduling primitives with explicit ownership, context, idempotency hooks, retry policy and lifecycle behavior, without pre-building the later durable event/job fabric.

## Implemented scope

- deterministic job identity/type registry with safe invalid/duplicate/unknown failures;
- UUIDv7 execution identity;
- synchronous and bounded queued execution for kernel-local background work;
- bounded worker concurrency and queue capacity;
- graceful shutdown covering accepted queued and synchronous work;
- cancellation/deadline propagation and deadline-triggered active-work cancellation;
- bounded retry/backoff requiring explicit idempotency for retried work;
- in-memory duplicate/conflict/in-progress protection;
- repeatable in-memory completion handles;
- deterministic one-shot/fixed-interval UTC-normalized kernel-maintenance schedules;
- correlation/observability propagation through completed P01.07 boundaries without private job-data leakage;
- panic containment and deterministic/race-oriented test harness;
- P01.01-P01.08 completed-package regression preservation;
- repository-wide Go quality verification;
- GitHub-hosted G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence.

## Out of scope retained

- NATS/JetStream durable streams and event consumers (`P04` phase);
- transactional outbox/inbox (`P04`);
- distributed workflow timers (`P05`);

- identity/tenancy/module/event/workflow/business implementation;
- AI/LLM/model gateway, planner, memory/RAG, agents or autonomous execution.

## Reliability & security invariants retained

Job handlers cannot infer authority from scheduler identity. Future tenant/actor context must be explicit and revalidated where required. Retries do not silently duplicate protected side effects. Shutdown stops accepting work and provides bounded drain/cancel behavior. Poison/failure semantics remain explicit with no infinite retry loops. Scheduler/job identity is operational identity, not authorization, tenancy or business authority. P01.09 did not create a durable messaging fabric or alternate business-state store.
