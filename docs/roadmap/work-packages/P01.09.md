# P01.09 — Job & Scheduler Primitives

- Phase: `P01`
- State: `planned`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.jobs`
- Depends on: `P01.08 done`
- Blocks: `P01.10`–`P01.12`
- Change class: `B`

> Specification-only while `kernel_code_authorized=false`.

## Objective

Provide minimal background job and scheduling primitives with explicit ownership, context, idempotency hooks, retry policy and lifecycle behavior, without pre-building the later durable event/job fabric.

## In scope

- job identity/type registry boundary;
- enqueue/execute result model for kernel-local background work;
- bounded worker concurrency and graceful shutdown;
- cancellation/deadline propagation;
- retry/backoff policy metadata;
- idempotency-key hook and duplicate-safe handler contract;
- schedule definition for simple recurring/one-shot kernel maintenance work;
- correlation/observability propagation;
- deterministic in-memory/test harness.

## Out of scope

- NATS/JetStream durable streams and event consumers (`P04` phase);
- transactional outbox/inbox (`P04`);
- distributed workflow timers (`P05`);
- business jobs;
- tenant-context implementation before P02, though the API must allow future explicit scope propagation.

## Reliability & security invariants

Job handlers cannot infer authority from scheduler identity. Future tenant/actor context must be explicit and revalidated where required. Retries must not silently duplicate protected side effects. Shutdown must stop accepting work and allow bounded drain/cancel behavior. Poison/failure semantics are explicit; no infinite retry loops.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Job registration/execution is deterministic and unknown jobs fail safely.
2. Retry/backoff is bounded and testable.
3. Duplicate/idempotency contract has executable positive and negative tests.
4. Graceful shutdown/drain/cancel behavior is proven.
5. Correlation context propagates into observability.
6. No durable messaging/workflow/business semantics are introduced.
7. Applicable G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence is PASS.

## Completion evidence

Record duplicate/retry/shutdown/cancellation tests, source SHA, workflow run/job and runner.

## State transition

`planned -> active` only after P01.08 `done`; completion unlocks only P01.10.
