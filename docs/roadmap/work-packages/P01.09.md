# P01.09 — Job & Scheduler Primitives

- Phase: `P01`
- State: `active`
- Owner/domain: `kernel.jobs`
- Depends on: `P01.08 done`
- Blocks: `P01.10`–`P01.12`
- Change class: `B`

> **Implementation authorization:** `kernel_code_authorized=true` for P01.09 only. `business_feature_code_authorized=false`. P01.09 is the sole active executable kernel scope after the governed P01.08 closure transition.

## Objective

Provide minimal background job and scheduling primitives with explicit ownership, context, idempotency hooks, retry policy and lifecycle behavior, without pre-building the later durable event/job fabric.

## In scope

- job identity/type registry boundary;
- enqueue/execute result model for kernel-local background work;
- bounded worker concurrency and graceful shutdown;
- cancellation/deadline propagation;
- retry/backoff policy metadata;
- idempotency-key hook and duplicate-safe handler contract;
- schedule definition for simple recurring/one-shot kernel maintenance work;
- correlation/observability propagation through completed P01.07 boundaries;
- deterministic in-memory/test harness;
- P01.01-P01.08 completed-package regression preservation;
- repository-wide Go quality verification;
- GitHub-hosted G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence.

## Out of scope

P01.09 must not implement:

- NATS/JetStream durable streams and event consumers (`P04` phase);
- transactional outbox/inbox (`P04`);
- distributed workflow timers (`P05`);
- business jobs;
- tenant-context implementation before P02, though API shape may allow future explicit scope propagation;
- feature flag/configuration registry owned by P01.10;
- audit transport owned by P01.11;
- developer CLI owned by P01.12;
- identity/tenancy/module/event/workflow/business implementation;
- AI/LLM/model gateway, planner, memory/RAG, agents or autonomous execution.

## Reliability & security invariants

Job handlers cannot infer authority from scheduler identity. Future tenant/actor context must be explicit and revalidated where required. Retries must not silently duplicate protected side effects. Shutdown must stop accepting work and allow bounded drain/cancel behavior. Poison/failure semantics are explicit; no infinite retry loops. Scheduler/job identity is operational identity, not authorization, tenancy or business authority. P01.09 must not create a durable messaging fabric or alternate business-state store.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Job registration/execution is deterministic and unknown jobs fail safely.
2. Retry/backoff is bounded and testable.
3. Duplicate/idempotency contract has executable positive and negative tests.
4. Graceful shutdown/drain/cancel behavior is proven.
5. Correlation context propagates into observability.
6. No durable messaging/workflow/business semantics are introduced.
7. Completed P01.01-P01.08 regression verifiers remain PASS.
8. Repository-wide Go quality remains PASS.
9. Applicable G0/G1/G2/G3/G5/G6/G7 evidence is PASS on GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.

## Completion evidence

Record registration/unknown-job, duplicate/idempotency, retry/backoff, bounded concurrency, shutdown/drain/cancellation and correlation/observability tests, source SHA, workflow run/job, executing runner, completed-package regressions and merge SHA.

`FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT RUN` or `N/A` cannot be relabeled PASS for required P01.09 gates.
