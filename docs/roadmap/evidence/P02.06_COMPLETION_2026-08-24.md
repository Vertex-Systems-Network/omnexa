## Delivered contract

The accepted P02.06 implementation provides only the governed relationship/context-aware authorization layer under `kernel.authorization`:

- accepted P02.05 direct RBAC remains mandatory and is evaluated before contextual policy;
- a dependency-inverted relationship resolver provides current trusted principal/object relationship evidence rather than treating caller-selected identifiers as authority;
- relationship evidence must match the exact principal, object, tenant and optional organization scope requested by the governed action;
- contextual constraints may narrow authority after RBAC + relationship success but cannot grant where an earlier check failed;
- interactive, internal and background caller origin is descriptive and never bypasses authorization;
- ordinary read, sensitive-field and export operations require distinct stable permissions where configured;
- material denials and privileged sensitive/export allows emit classification-safe required audit records using the existing P01.11 boundary;
- resolver/evaluator dependency errors fail closed with safe structured authorization failures;
- deterministic unit/security tests cover same-scope allow, missing permission, wrong tenant, wrong organization, wrong object, absent relationship, contextual narrowing, internal/background non-bypass, sensitive/export separation, audit safety and dependency failure;
- PostgreSQL integration composes the contextual layer with accepted P02.05 RBAC persistence.

P02.06 introduced no new authoritative persistence or migration. Object relationship facts remain owned by their authoritative domain and are consumed through the injected resolver. No P02.07 MFA/passkeys, P02.08 service accounts/API credentials, P03 module permission registry/runtime, business-domain write ownership, business features/UI, deployment authority or AI/model/agent runtime was implemented.
