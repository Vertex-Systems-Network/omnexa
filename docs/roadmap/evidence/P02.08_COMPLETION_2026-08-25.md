## Delivered contract

The accepted P02.08 implementation provides only the governed non-human service-principal and API-credential foundation:

- a distinct Service Account aggregate under `kernel.identity`, reusing canonical principal type/lifecycle semantics without representing automation as a human User;
- exact immutable tenant/optional-organization credential binding ceilings;
- one-time high-entropy API credential issuance with SHA-256 verifier-only persistence;
- current-state credential verification with expiry, revocation and supersession checks;
- transactional credential rotation that invalidates the superseded credential before committing the replacement;
- explicit credential revocation and classification-safe last-used/rotation metadata;
- direct service-account RBAC composition through `kernel.authorization`, without moving permission authority into identity or credential possession;
- exact tenant/organization role-assignment and permission checks against current principal, tenant and assignment state;
- additive `kernel.identity` v4 and `kernel.authorization` v2 migrations with fresh/idempotent and supported-upgrade evidence;
- redacted formatting/JSON shapes and no raw-secret persistence/logging/audit/error paths;
- deterministic allowed-scope plus wrong-tenant, wrong-organization, wrong-permission, revoked, expired and superseded negative evidence.

P02.08 does not implement OAuth developer applications, connector/provider integrations, device/POS identities, AI-agent execution identity, P02.09 tenant settings, P02.10 phase-exit behavior, business API scopes/features/UI, deployment authority or generic platform superkeys/master tokens.
