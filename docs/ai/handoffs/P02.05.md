## Objective

Implement the deny-by-default RBAC foundation described in `docs/roadmap/work-packages/P02.05.md` on the trusted identity, tenant and organization foundations without role-name shortcuts or relationship/context policy reserved for P02.06.

## Authorized boundary

- owner: `kernel.authorization`;
- stable permission identifiers and governed capability-oriented permission semantics;
- Role as a deterministic permission composition, never a bypass identity;
- tenant/organization-scoped role definitions and assignments where authorized;
- assignment/revocation lifecycle and deterministic evaluation of direct RBAC grants;
- privileged role/permission mutation checks and classification-safe audit hooks;
- deterministic allow, deny and privilege-escalation negative tests;
- applicable owner-bounded persistence, migrations and focused authorization verification.

## Security invariants

- deny by default;
- role names such as `admin`, `owner` or `superuser` never mean platform bypass;
- protected role/permission mutations authorize server-side;
- role assignment itself is privileged and auditable;
- tenant/organization identifiers cannot be substituted to widen authority;
- P02.01 identity, P02.02 tenancy, P02.03 organization and P02.04 authentication/session ownership boundaries remain intact;
- bearer/session authentication does not itself grant RBAC authority.

## Forbidden scope

- P02.07 MFA/passkey requirements;
- P02.08 service-account/API credential scope;
- P02.09 tenant-scoped settings;
- P02.10 phase-exit audit product behavior;
- P03 module permission registration/runtime;
- business-module permissions or hidden super-admin behavior;
- business features/UI;
- deployment/Kubernetes authority;
- AI/model/agent runtime.
