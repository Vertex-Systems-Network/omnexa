## P01.11 objective

Provide the protected audit transport/envelope boundary for future security and business audit records without conflating audit with ordinary logs or prematurely implementing P02 identities/business audit catalogs.

## Forbidden scope

Do not implement P02 identity/tenant/role audit catalogs, business-domain audit event definitions, compliance export/reporting UI, long-term legal retention/hold, replacement of domain events with audit records, P01.12 developer CLI, durable messaging/outbox/inbox pull-forward, later business modules or AI/model/agent runtime.

Audit write capability does not imply audit read/export authority. Actor/scope identifiers are descriptive metadata only and do not grant authorization, identity or tenancy authority. Required-audit sink failure must not silently claim success, and protected audit remains separate from ordinary logs.
