## Retained semantic laws

- duplicate delivery can occur, especially around crash/restart boundaries;
- no global ordering guarantee is assumed;
- checkpoint state represents consumption progress, not authorization or business success;
- event identity/correlation/causation/tenant/classification metadata remains authoritative and unchanged;
- trusted tenant context is validated fail-closed;
- an event, checkpoint token, transport offset or consumer ID is not an authorization credential;
- protected consumers remain application-idempotent;
- provider exactly-once claims cannot substitute for duplicate-safe application evidence;
- stale/regressive/conflicting checkpoint attempts fail deterministically;
- failed or cancelled unacknowledged work cannot be silently skipped by progress advancement;
- owner/tenant/scope collisions fail closed rather than being merged or rebound implicitly.
