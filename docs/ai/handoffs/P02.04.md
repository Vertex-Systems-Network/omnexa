## Objective

Implement only the authentication and interactive session lifecycle described in `docs/roadmap/work-packages/P02.04.md`, building on the completed identity, trusted tenant and organization-scope foundations without treating authentication as authorization.

## Authorized boundary

- owner: `kernel.identity`;
- authentication mechanism boundary and approved adaptive password hashing where passwords are supported;
- session/access/refresh credential lifecycle with explicit expiry, rotation and revocation;
- short-lived access credentials relative to refresh/session credentials;
- device/session inventory semantics where supported;
- deterministic invalidation after material account/security changes according to policy;
- tenant/organization context re-authorization rather than trusting stale client context;
- disclosure-safe authentication failures and synthetic security fixtures;
- audit hooks for security-significant lifecycle changes without secret payloads;
- applicable classification-safe persistence, fresh/upgrade migrations and focused security tests.

## Security invariants

- authentication proves principal identity; it does not grant business authority;
- passwords are never plaintext or reversibly stored;
- refresh/session secrets are `RESTRICTED` and never logged, traced, returned in errors or placed into audit payloads;
- bearer possession cannot bypass current authorization/policy state;
- tenant and organization selectors/context must be revalidated against authoritative current state;
- reset/recovery flows remain authentication flows rather than ordinary profile updates;
- P02.01 identity, P02.02 tenancy and P02.03 organization ownership boundaries remain intact.

## Forbidden scope

- P02.05-P02.06 RBAC/policy implementation;
- P02.07 MFA/passkey factors;
- P02.08 service-account/API credential lifecycle;
- P02.09 tenant-scoped settings;
- P02.10 phase-exit audit product behavior;
- SAML/SCIM/enterprise SSO (`P24`);
- business login portals/UI or business features;
- P03 module runtime, deployment/Kubernetes authority or AI/model/agent runtime.

## Acceptance direction

The existing P02.04 acceptance criteria remain unchanged:

1. valid authentication/session issuance follows explicit lifecycle/expiry rules;
2. invalid credentials do not disclose unnecessary account existence/details;
3. rotation/revocation and required session invalidation are deterministic and tested;
4. tokens/secrets are absent from logs, traces, errors and audit payloads;
5. stale tenant/org context cannot become authorization authority;
6. no RBAC/MFA/service-account/business scope is pulled forward;
7. applicable fresh/upgrade migrations, Go quality and prior regressions pass;
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
