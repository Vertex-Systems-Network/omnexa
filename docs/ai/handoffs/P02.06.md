## Objective

Implement the relationship/context-aware authorization layer described in `docs/roadmap/work-packages/P02.06.md`, combining accepted P02.05 direct RBAC with trusted tenant/organization relationships, object scope and contextual policy checks at governed capability boundaries.

## Authorized boundary

- owner: `kernel.authorization`;
- relationship/context policy evaluation layered on accepted P02.05 RBAC;
- trusted tenant, organization and object-scope relationships;
- capability-bound authorization decisions with deny-by-default semantics;
- contextual conditions that cannot grant outside the principal's valid relationships;
- field/export distinction hooks where broader read authority must not imply sensitive-field/export authority;
- disclosure-safe deny behavior and classification-safe material authorization audit hooks;
- deterministic same-scope allow plus wrong-tenant/wrong-org/wrong-object/missing-permission negatives;
- applicable owner-bounded persistence, migrations and focused authorization verification.

## Security invariants

- authorization remains deny by default and builds on, rather than bypasses, P02.05 RBAC;
- client IDs, tenant IDs, organization IDs and object IDs are references, never authority;
- tenant membership alone is insufficient for all child scopes;
- contextual policy cannot widen authority beyond current trusted principal/scope relationships;
- role names such as `admin`, `owner` or `superuser` never mean platform bypass;
- internal/background caller origin never bypasses authorization policy;
- field/export authorization may be stricter than ordinary resource read;
- P02.01 identity, P02.02 tenancy, P02.03 organization, P02.04 authentication/session and P02.05 direct-RBAC ownership boundaries remain intact.

## Forbidden scope

- P02.07 MFA/passkey requirements;
- P02.08 service-account/API credential lifecycle;
- P02.09 tenant-scoped settings;
- P02.10 phase-exit product behavior;
- P03 module permission registration/capability registry;
- business-domain object policies whose owners are not active;
- support impersonation product surface;
- business features/UI;
- deployment/Kubernetes authority;
- AI/model/agent runtime.

## Acceptance direction

The frozen P02.06 acceptance criteria remain unchanged:

1. a valid relationship/context plus required permission allows the governed action;
2. wrong tenant, wrong organization, wrong object relationship and missing permission deny deterministically;
3. role names and internal-call origin cannot bypass policy;
4. contextual rules never widen beyond trusted principal/scope relationships;
5. material denials/privileged decisions are auditable without leaking sensitive data where applicable;
6. no P03/business/AI authorization surface is introduced;
7. Go quality, migrations where affected and all prior regressions pass;
8. applicable G0-G8 evidence runs on canonical GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-24.04`.
