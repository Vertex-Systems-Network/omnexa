- tenant-scoped and approved organization-scoped setting resolution;

- `kernel.configuration` remains authoritative owner;
- no cross-tenant fallback or global-write shortcut;
- P02.01-P02.08 identity, tenancy, organization, authentication, authorization, strong-auth and service-account boundaries remain intact.
